## ✦ About Me

I'm **Mokshagna Tej**, a second-year Computer Science undergraduate at **Vel Tech University**, and I like sitting right at the intersection of engineering and design. Most days that means shipping full-stack features in the **MERN stack**, and most nights it means grinding through **DSA problems** in Java, C, and C++ until they finally click.

Lately my focus has been **Cloudwatch Server Anomaly**, a full-stack anomaly prediction app, and gearing up for **Microsoft Imagine Cup 2026**, where I'm building an AI-powered solution end to end using Azure and the OpenAI API. I got my first taste of real-world engineering as a **software development intern at Innolift Ventures**, working under Track-3.

Outside the codebase, I moonlight as a **page and editorial designer** for my department's magazine, and I run my entire academic life through a custom-built **Notion system** — trackers, roadmaps, and databases included. I care as much about how something looks as I do about how it works.
